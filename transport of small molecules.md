mode of transport:
- relying on *concentration gradient* (passive):
	- [[simple diffusion]] - through membrane
		- simple diffusion of water called [[osmosis]]
	- [[facilitated diffusion]] - though [[transport protein]]
		- facilitated diffusion of water via aquaporin protein
- defy *concentration gradient* (active):
	- [[primary active transport]]
		- directly uses ATPase to transport eg. 
			[[Na-K ATPase]], [[proton-K ATPase]]
	- [[secondary active transport]]
		- uses one ATPase to create concentration gradient for another transport eg.
			SGLT1 (Sodium-Glucose cotransporter)
