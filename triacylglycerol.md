or triglyceride, TG
most common type of lipid
structure:
- [[glycerol]]
- 3 [[fatty acid]]
function:
- energy storage in adipose tissue
- provide essential fatty acid from diet
- protect vital organ
- thermal insulator

storage:
- [[adipocyte]]
	- [[lipoprotein lipase]] hydrolyze TG in chylomicron and VLCL creating free FA
	- adipocyte uptake free fatty acid and recreate TG 
	- stimulated by insulin in [[fed state]]
- [[hepatocyte]]
	- glycerol uptake to synthesize TG in [[fed state]]

precursor:
- [[glucose]] -> [[DHAP]] -> [[G3P]]
- fatty acid acyl-coA

lipolysis - at adipocyte
- controlled by hormone sensitive lipase
- produce free FA into blood + albumin
