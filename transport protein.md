types
- channel protein - substances pass through
	- ion channel
		- non-gated (leak)
		- gated
			- voltage-gated
			- ligand-gated
		- mechanical
- carrier protein - binds to substance

types of transport:
- [[uniport]] - transporting one type of molecule in one direction
- [[symport]] - transporting two types of molecules in same direction
- [[antiport]] - transporting two types of molecules in opposite direction
	eg. Na-K ATPase, proton-K ATPase
