water diffuses through membrane from low concentration (more water) to high concentration (less water)

types of concentration
- hypotonic solution
	- higher conc. outside
	- less water outside -> water moves out more
	- cell shrinks (shriveled)
- isotonic solution
	- equal concentration -> water moves out = water moves in
	- cell normal
- hypotonic solution
	- lower conc. outside
	- more water outside -> water moves in more
	- cell swells (lysed)
