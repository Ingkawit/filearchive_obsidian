occurs in mitochondria (might go to peroxisome first for shortening)
*no fatty acid catabolism:* RBC (no mitochondria), brain (FA cannot pass blood-brain barrier)
produce **acetyl coA**, NADH, FADH2 
requires 2ATP (for adding coA)

| size  | carbons | site of catabolism         | membrane transport             | activation of FA (adding coA) |
| ----- | ------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| SCFA  | 2-6     | mitochondria               | diffusion                      | cytosol or mitochondria       |
| MCFA  | 7-12    | mitochondria               | diffusion                      | mitochondrial matrix          |
| LCFA  | 13-22   | mitochondria               | carnitine shuttle (CPTI/CPTII) | outer mitochondrial membrane  |
| VLCFA | >22     | peroxisome -> mitochondria | peroxisomal transport          | peroxisome                    |
[[Zellweger syndrome]] - defective peroxisomal enzyme -> accumulation of VLCFA 

types of oxidation:
- [[beta-oxidation]]
- [[omega-oxidation]]
