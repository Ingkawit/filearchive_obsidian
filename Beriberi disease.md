
types:
- Wet beriberi (acute)
	- dilated cardiomyopathy
	- high output cardiac and biventricular failure
	- pitting edema (บวมกดบุ๋ม)
	- dyspnea, tachypnea
- Dry beriberi (chronic)
	- peripheral nervous system
		- muscle wasting, ชาแขนขา
	- central nervous system
		- Wernicke's encephalopathy
			- confusion, ataxia (เดินเซ), opthalmoplegia(กรอกตา)
		- Korsakoff's syndrome
			- psychosis (memory loss)
			- confabulation (deja vu)
