a protein poison produced by *Vibrio cholerae*
mechanism:
- binds to GM1 receptor to trigger [[receptor-mediated endocytosis]]
- part A of toxin triggers [[adenylate cyclase]] enzyme which produce [[cyclic AMP]] -> protein kinase A -> [[CFTR protein channel]]
- CFTR channel allows Cl to rush outside, water is forced to follow salt
- watery diarrhea
