most are hydropholic -> binds to cell-surface receptor
some are hydrophobic and bound to carrier protein -> binds to intracellular receptor

types:
- [[hormone]] - substances in trace amount traveling via blood stream to target cells
- [[growth factor]] - polypeptides promoting cellular poliferation
- [[cytokine]] - low molecular weight regulatory proteins, primarily secreted by leukocytes
- [[neurotransmitter]] - hydrophilic molecules that bind to receptor in nerve cells
- gas - diffuse across plasma membrane eg. nitric oxide
