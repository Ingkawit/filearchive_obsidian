programmed cell death

triggered by [[cytochrome C]] from mitochondria

*Caenorhabditis elegans* (round worm) apoptotic genes
- ced-3, ced-3 trigger cell death
- ced-9 inhibits cell death
Mammalian apoptotic genes
- ced-3 homologous to ICE ([[caspase]]) (trigger cell death)
- ced-9 homologous to [[BCL-2]] (inhibits cell death)

stimuli (stress)
- UV light
- chemical
- viral infection
- growth factor deprivation
- oxidative stress
- death ligand
**extrinsic pathway (death receptor)**
- death ligand binds to death receptor on cell membrane
- pro-caspase 8 or 10 form death-inducing signaling complex (DISC)
- activation of caspase 8 or 10
**intrinsic pathway**
- mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization (MOMP) 
	- release of [[cytochrome C]] though outer membrane by BCL-2 family (BAX and BAK)
	- cytochrome C, Apaf1, pro-capase 9 form apoptosome 
	- activation of caspase 9 (initiator caspase)
**execution phase**
- caspase 8 or 10 and caspase 9 activate caspase 3 (executioner caspase)
- caspase 3 destroy cytoskeleton, activate DNAase
- cell dies

steps:
0. more phosphatidylserine on outer cell membrane (Eat me signal)
1. cell shrinkage
2. chromatin condensation (pyknosis)
3. cell membrane forms small bulges (blebbing)
4. fragmentation of DNA and cell (karyorrhexis), forming apoptotic bodies
5. phagocytosis by nearby phagocyte


detecting apoptosis
- DNA laddering assay 
- TUNEL - detect 3'-OH end

too much apoptosis
- [[AIDS]] 
- neurodegenerative diseases eg. [[Alzheimer's disease]], [[Parkinson's disease]]
too little apoptosis
- cancer
- autoimmune disorders

preventing apoptosis
- inhibitor of apoptosis protein (IAP)
- caspase inhibitor
- BCL-2 modulator
	- BH-3 mimetic - anticancer drug
