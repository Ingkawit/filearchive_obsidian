to regulate its own or another cell's growth, coordinate movement, control metabolism, make other cells die, maintenance of homeostasis

extracellular -> receptor protein -> intracellular signals -> effector protein -> response 
slow if involve changes in gene expression

types:
- juxtacrine - cells contact each other
- paracrine - nearby cells via signaling molecules eg. [[neurotransmitter]]
- endocrine - signaling molecule travel through blood stream
- autocrine - self stimulation

receptors:
- cell-surface receptor
	- ions-channel-coupled (transmitter-gated) receptor 
	- G-protein-coupled receptor
- intracellular receptor
	- nuclear receptor
