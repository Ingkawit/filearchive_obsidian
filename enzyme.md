usually [[protein]] that ccatalyzes chemical reaction
works with [[cofactor]] and coenzyme

mechanism:
- reduce activation energy 

model for enzyme
- lock and key (obsolete) - totally matched 
- induced-fit 

substrate specificty:
- geometric specificity - specific to chemical groups 
- stereo specificty - specific to isomers (stereoisomer)

reaction types:
- **oxidoreductase** - redox reaction eg. ribonucleotide reductase, dehydrogenase
- **transferase** - transfer chemical group eg. kinase, transaminase
- **hydrolase** - hydrolysis (break bond with water) eg. protease
- **lyase** - break bond with other methods eg. pyruvate decarboxylase
- **isomerase** - switch isomers eg. triose phosphate isomerase
- **ligase/synthase** - bond two molecules eg. DNA ligase, DNA polymerase

**[[enzyme kinetics]]**

[[inhibition]]

factors:
- substrate concentration
- temperature
	- too low - not enough energy
	- optimal temperature
	- too high - enzyme (protein) denature
- pH
- isozyme
	- similar enzymes with amino acid sequence altered causing change in properties
- proenzyme/zymogen
	- inactive precursor 
	- activate by altering amino acid sequences
	- eg. prothrombin, protrypsin
- multienzyme complex
	- a complex of many enzymes working in continuous process
	- eg. fatty acid synthase
- phosphorylation/dephosphorylation
	- phosphorylation enzyme called kinase
	- dephosphorylation enzyme called phosphatase
- [[allosteric control]]
	- activator or inhibitor binds to allosteric site
	- cooperative allosteric transition
	- modulator protein
