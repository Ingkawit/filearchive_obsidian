rate limiting step: **carnitine acyltransferase I (CATI)** or **carnitine palmitoyl transferase I (CPTI)**
transport completed by translocase and CPTII at inner membrane

oxidation of beta carbon by cleaving between alpha and beta carbon

#### even chain
yield per cycle : 1 acetyl coA, 1 NADH, 1 FADH2 
key enzyme: acyl-coA dehydrogenase (FADH2) , beta-hydroxyl-coA dehydrogenase (NADH)
$$\text{rounds of } \beta  \text{-oxidation = } \frac{carbons}{2}-1$$
##### odd chain
last round : acetyl coA + propionyl coA -> succinyl coA -> OAA -> glucose (via gluconeogenesis)
key enzyme: propionyl-coA carboxylase
coenzyme: biotine (B7)
- clinical:
	- biotin deficiency -> high propionyl coA -> acidemia, aciduria

clinical 
- primary carnitine deficeincy -> cannot transport carnitine into cell
	- low FA -> hypoketotic, hypoglycemia
	- muscle damage
	- treatment : oral carnitine supplement, high carb low fat, avoid fasting
- secondary carnitine defiency (CPT or CAT defect) -> FA cannot enter mitochondria
	- CPT1 defiency -> not enough metabolism -> hypoglycemia
	- CPT2 defiency ->  accumulation of long chain acylcarnitine -> cardiac arrest
	- treatment: high carb diet, MCFA supplement (no need carnitine shuttle), avoid fasting
