derived from [[arachidonic acid]] (20 carbon FA)

property:
- autocrine and paracrine mediator
- very unstable, synthesized on demand
- regulate inflammation, immune response

function:
- prostaglandins (PGE,PGF) - from endothelial cell
	- vasodilation
	- vasoconstriction
- prostacyclin - from endothelial cell
	- inhibit platelet aggregation
- thromboxane - from platelet
	- promote platelet aggregation
- leukotriene from leukocyte
	- mediate bronchoconstriction, inflammation
