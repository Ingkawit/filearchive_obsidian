First law of thermodynamics
- total energy is conserved
- total heat of a system at constant pressure called **enthalpy** (∆H)
Second law of thermodynamics
- the reaction will proceed in the direction that increases chaos called **entropy** (energetically favorable )
- movement toward non-spontaneous reaction requires energy
	- energetically unfavorable reaction can happen by coupling with energetically favorable reaction
		- eg. energetically favorable reaction (hydrolysis of [[ATP]]) is coupled with other reactions or used to join two molecules
- [[Gibbs's free energy]]
