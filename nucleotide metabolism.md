
[[nucleotide]]

purine (2 rings)
- de novo synthesis
	- from PRPP (ribose) to IMP (inosinic acid)
	- involve THF ([[folate (B9)]]) as single-carbon carrier
	- aspartate -> fumarate to donate amino group
	- anti-folate drugs inhibits synthesis 
- salvage - recycle from nucleotide
	- involve phosphoribosyl transferase

pyrimidine (1 ring)
- de novo 
	- involve CPS-II
- salvage

rNDP (ADP, CDP, UDP, GDP) to dNDP
- rNDP reductase
	- ATP binds at A site to activate, dATP binds at A site to deactivate
		- ATP binds S site will favor catalyzing CDP, UDP
		- dTTP binds S site will favor catalyzing GDP, ADP
		- dGTP binds S site will favor catalyzing ADP
purine degradation
- creates hypoxanthine -> xanthine -> uric acid
pyrimidine degradation
- uracil creates beta-alanine 
	- beta-alanine 
		- -> [[pantothenic acid (B5)]]
		- -> malonyl coA
		- -> acetyl coA

clinical:
- orotic aciduria
- pyrimidine-5'-nucleptidase deficiency
- [[gouty arthritis]]
- Lesch-Nyhan syndrome
	- X-linked recessive
	- defect in HGRT
	- hyperuricemia, mental retardation
- adenosine deaminase deficiency
	- accumulation of dATP -> inhibit rNDP reductase
	- leads to SCIDs

drugs
- fluorouracil
	- inhibit thymidylate synthase (dUMP -> dTMP) part of folate cycle
- methptrexate
	- THF analog
- 6-mercaptopurine
	- inhibit de novo purine synthesis
- zidovudine - anti-HIV drug
	- nucleoside analog
- [[acyclovir]] - anti-HSV drug
	- specific to infected cell
