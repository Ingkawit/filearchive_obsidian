## 1. Macromolecules (foundation)

- **[[macromolecules]]** — built from monomers via covalent bonds; assemble into supramolecular structures via noncovalent bonds
    - **[[carbohydrate]]** → monosaccharide → [[disaccharide]] → [[polysaccharide]] (glycogen, starch, GAGs, glycoprotein)
    - **[[protein]]** → [[amino acid]] → functions: enzyme, structural (actin/tubulin), motor (dynein), transport, receptor/signaling
    - **[[lipid]]** → fatty acid/glycerol → glycerolipid, sphingolipid, steroid, isoprenoid
    - **[[nucleic acid]]** → [[nucleotide]] → [[DNA]] / [[RNA]]

---

## 2. Cell Organelles

- **[[organelle]]** — eukaryote vs prokaryote comparison
- **Double membrane-bound:** [[plasma membrane]], [[nucleus]] (+ [[nucleolus]]), [[mitochondria]]
- **Single membrane-bound:**
    - **[[endoplasmic reticulum]]** → [[rough endoplasmic reticulum]] (protein synthesis/modification) vs [[smooth endoplasmic reticulum]] (lipid/steroid synthesis, detox, Ca²⁺ storage)
    - **[[Golgi apparatus]]** — cis (entry) → trans (exit); sorts to lysosome/secretion/membrane
    - **[[lysosome]]** — acidic hydrolases (M6P tagging pathway); autophagy/endocytosis → _storage diseases_
    - **[[peroxisome]]** — VLCFA β-oxidation, bile acid synthesis, H₂O₂ detox via catalase → _Zellweger/ALD_
- **Non membrane-bound:** [[ribosome]] ([[free ribosome]] vs [[ER-bound ribosome]]), **[[proteasome]]** (ubiquitin-tagged degradation), [[centrosome]], [[cytoskeleton]]

---

## 3. Cytoskeleton & Motility

- **[[cytoskeleton]]** overview — plus/minus end dynamics
    - **[[microtubule]]** — tubulin dimers, dynamic instability (catastrophe/rescue), [[centrosome]], [[microtubule-associated proteins]] (Tau), [[microtubule-specific drugs]] (colchicine, taxol)
    - **[[microfilmament]]** (actin) — G-actin/F-actin, structures (cortex, stress fiber, lamellipodium, filopodium), [[actin-specific inhibitor]] (phalloidin, cytochalasin, latrunculin)
    - **[[intermediate filament]]** — no plus/minus ends, [[keratin]], forms [[nuclear lamina]]
    - **[[motor protein]]** — kinesin/dynein (microtubule) vs myosin (actin)
    - **[[cell motility]]** — microtubule-based (cilia/flagella) vs actin-based (crawling, lamellipodium, Arp2/3)
- **Clinical:** [[spherocytosis]] (cytoskeletal membrane defect)

---

## 4. Plasma Membrane & Transport

- **[[plasma membrane]]** — fluid mosaic model, fluidity factors, selective permeability
- **[[membrane protein]]** → [[transport protein]] (channel vs carrier; uniport/symport/antiport)
- **[[transport of small molecules]]**
    - Passive: [[simple diffusion]] ([[osmosis]] for water), [[facilitated diffusion]]
    - Active: [[primary active transport]] (Na-K ATPase, proton-K ATPase), secondary active transport (SGLT1)
- **[[bulk transport]]** → [[endocytosis]] (phagocytosis, pinocytosis, [[receptor-mediated endocytosis]]) / exocytosis
- **Clinical:** [[cystic fibrosis]] (CFTR defect), [[cholera toxin]] mechanism, COVID-19 (ACE-2 receptor-mediated entry)

---

## 5. Cell Junctions, Adhesion & ECM

- **[[cell junction]]** / **[[cell adhesion]]**
    - [[tight junction]] (claudins/occludins) — epithelial barrier
    - [[adherens junction]] (cadherin–actin)
    - [[desmosome junction]] (cadherin–intermediate filament) — cardiac/skin
    - [[gap junction]] (connexons) — cell communication
    - [[cell-to-matrix junction]] (integrin) — actin-linked vs hemidesmosome (intermediate filament)
- **[[extracellular matrix]]** — collagen, elastic fiber, proteoglycan, GAGs, laminin, integrin; specialized as [[basal lamina]]
- **Clinical:** Marfan syndrome, epidermolysis bullosa, ARVC (desmosome defect)

---

## 6. DNA — Structure, Replication, Repair, Recombination

- **[[DNA]]** structure → **[[chromatin]]** (DNA + histone) → **[[chromosome]]** (hetero- vs euchromatin)
- **[[DNA replication]]** — semiconservative, S phase, 5'→3'
    - [[replisome]]: helicase, SSB protein, [[DNA primase]], [[DNA polymerase]] (III & I), clamp/topoisomerase, [[DNA ligase]]
    - [[replication fork]] — leading vs lagging (Okazaki fragments)
    - Telomere maintenance — [[telomerase]], [[reverse transcriptase]]
- **[[DNA damage]]** — ROS, UV (pyrimidine dimers), radiation (strand breaks), deamination, depurination
- **[[DNA repair]]** pathways (match damage type):
    - [[base excision repair]] — single-strand break
    - [[nucleotide excision repair]] — pyrimidine dimer/intrastrand crosslink
    - [[mismatch repair]] — base mismatch
    - [[homologous recombination]] & [[non-homologous end joining]] — double-strand break
    - [[SOS repair]]
    - Clinical: xeroderma pigmentosum, BRCA1/2
- **[[DNA recombination]]** types — homologous recombination, NHEJ, [[site-specific recombination]], [[transposition]]
- **[[DNA mutation]]** — point (missense/nonsense/silent), insertion/deletion (frameshift), germ-line vs somatic
- **[[chromosomal aberration]]** — deletion (Cri du Chat), duplication (trisomies 21/18/13, Klinefelter, Turner), translocation

## 7. Gene Expression — Transcription & RNA Processing

- **[[gene]]** structure (promoter, intron, exon) within **[[genome]]**
- **[[transcription]]** — steps (initiation/elongation/termination), prokaryote vs eukaryote comparison table
    - **[[cis regulatory elements]]** (promoter, operator, silencer, enhancer) vs **[[trans acting factors]]** (RNA pol, transcription factor, repressor/activator)
    - **[[RNA]]** types: mRNA, tRNA, rRNA, ncRNA
- **[[post-transcriptional processing]]** — 5' capping, [[splicing]] (spliceosome), 3' poly-A tail
    - Clinical: SLE (anti-snRNP), beta-thalassemia (splice-site mutation)
- **Prokaryotic regulation:** **[[operon]]** (lac operon — repressor, CAP/cAMP, catabolite repression)
- **Eukaryotic regulation:** **[[control of gene expression]]** — gene/transcription/post-transcription/translation levels; **[[epigenetics]]** (methylation, histone modification, ncRNA)

## 8. Translation & Protein Fate

- **[[translation]]** — initiation (P site), elongation (A→P→E sites), termination; prokaryote vs eukaryote differences
- **[[post-translational protein modification]]** — phosphorylation, carboxylation, methylation, glycosylation, ubiquitination
- **[[protein trafficking]]**
    - Gated transport: nuclear import/export ([[nuclear pore]], importin/exportin), peroxisome import (PEX)
    - Transmembrane: mitochondria (TOM/TIM), ER (SRP, cotranslational translocation)
    - Vesicular: coat proteins (COPI/COPII/clathrin), motor proteins (kinesin/dynein), SNAREs
- **[[protein degradation]]** — ubiquitin-proteasome pathway; alpha1-antitrypsin deficiency

---

## 9. Enzymes & Kinetics

- **[[enzyme]]** — mechanism (lowers activation energy), induced-fit model, specificity, 6 reaction classes (oxidoreductase, transferase, hydrolase, lyase, isomerase, ligase)
- **[[cofactor]]** / **[[coenzyme]]** — apoenzyme + coenzyme → holoenzyme
- **[[enzyme kinetics]]** — Michaelis-Menten (Km, Vmax, kcat), Lineweaver-Burk plot
- **[[inhibition]]**
    - Irreversible (covalent): organophosphates, iodoacetamide, penicillin
    - Reversible: [[competitive inhibition]] (↑Km, Vmax same), [[uncompetitive inhibition]] (↓Km, ↓Vmax), [[noncompetitive inhibition]] (Km same, ↓Vmax)
- **[[allosteric control]]** — feedback inhibition, [[cooperative allosteric transition]]

## 10. Water-Soluble Vitamins

- **[[water-soluble vitamin]]** overview — lab tests, heat/light stability, storage forms
- **B1 [[thiamine (B1)]]** — pyruvate/α-KG dehydrogenase coenzyme → [[B1 deficiency]] → Beriberi (wet: cardiac; dry: neuro — Wernicke/Korsakoff)
- **B2 [[riboflavin (B2)]]** — FMN/FAD → [[riboflavin(B2) deficiency]] (cheilosis, angular stomatitis, glossitis)
- **B3 [[niacin (B3)]]** — NAD/NADP → [[niacin(B3) deficiency]] (Pellagra: dermatitis, diarrhea, dementia) / [[niacin (B3) toxicity]] (flushing)
- **B5 [[pantothenic acid (B5)]]** — coenzyme A → [[pantothenic acid (B5) deficiency]] (burning foot syndrome)
- **B6 [[pyridoxine (B6)]]** — PLP, transamination, GABA synthesis, heme synthesis → [[pyridoxine (B6) deficiency]] (sideroblastic anemia, seizures)
- **B7 [[biotin (B7)]]** — carboxylation reactions → [[biotin (B7) deficiency]] (avidin in egg white; dermatitis)
- **B9 [[folate (B9)]]** — THF, single-carbon transfer → [[folate (B9) deficiency]] (megaloblastic anemia, neural tube defects)
- **B12 [[cobalamin (B12)]]** — IF-dependent absorption pathway → [[cobalamin (B12) deficiency]] (megaloblastic anemia + neuropathy; ↑MMA)
- **C [[ascorbic acid (C)]]** — collagen hydroxylation, antioxidant → [[ascorbic acid (C) deficiency]] (Scurvy)
- **Comparison:** [[anemia from B vitamins deficiency]] — B9/B12 (megaloblastic) vs B6 (microcytic hypochromic)
