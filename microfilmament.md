two intertwined strand of [[actin]]

functions:
- control cell shape 
	- cortex (cross-linked network at cell membrane)
	- stress fiber (anti-parallel contractile structure)
	- lamellipodium (branched cross-linked network)
	- filopodium (parallel bundle)
- **muscle contraction 
- cytoplasmic streaming
- [[cell motility ]]
- **cytokinesis** in cell division 
- forms [[microvili]]


structure
- G-actin (monomer) forms F-actin (polymer)
- 2 F-actins form actin filament

polymerization:
- ATP bound at center of G-actins polymerize at **plus end**
- connected G-actins become ADP
