or nicotinic acid
[[tryptophan]] amino acid is precursor

found in cereal, diary, corn (niacytin - bound form)

part of coenzymes:
- [[NADH]] nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide 
	- dehydrogenase
	- electron transport system
- [[NADPH]] nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate 
	- fatty acid synthesis
	- cholesterol synthesis
	- non essential amino acid synthesis
	- purine, pyrimidine synthesis

nicotinic acid can treat [[hypercholesterolemia]] and [[hypertriglyceridemia]]
