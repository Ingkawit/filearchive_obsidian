long, unbranched chain of repeated disaccharides
negative charged
found in cartilage, vitreous humor, etc.

types:
- glucose-glucose-derived disaccharide polymer
	- hyaluronan - shock absorption
	- heparin - anticoagulant
	- heparan sulfate - glumerular filtration
- glucose-galactose-derived disaccharide polymer
	- chondroitin sulfate - compression resistance
	- dermatan sulfate - coagulation
	- keratan sulfate - shock absorption
