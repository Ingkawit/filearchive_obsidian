[[central dogma]] 
synthesis of RNA (5’ to 3’)
template strand/antisense strand (3’ to 5’)
- both strands can be template
- [[promoter]] determines template 

[[cis regulatory elements]]: non-coding DNA sequence wanted gene
[[trans acting factors]]: proteins that bind to DNA or RNA and interact with cis-acting sites

steps:
1. initiation 
	- **RNA polymerase and sigma factor** bind to **promoter** (no primer needed)
2. elongation
	- RNA polymerase moves along template (5’ to 3’ of new RNA)
3. termination
	- euk.
		- RNA endonuclease cleave new RNA when recognize sequence AAUAA
	- prok.
		- RNA polymerase release when recognize G-C rich region followed by UUU
		- or rho protein binds to RNA strand to release

difference prok. vs eu.
- prok.
	- transcription and translation simultaneously 
	- no RNA processing
- eu.
	- transcription give pre-mature mRNA
	- needs RNA processing
