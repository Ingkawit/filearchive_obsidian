structure:
- central sterol made of 4 hydrocarbon rings and a hydroxyl group

function and property:
- non-caloric
- nonsaponifiable
- precursor of [[steroid hormone]]
- precursor of [[bile acid]], [[vitamin D]]
- cell membrane fluidity

input to liver cholesterol pool:
- diet -> [[chylomicron]]
- synthesized from outside liver -> [[HDL]]
- synthesized in liver

output from liver cholesterol pool:
- VLDL secretion
- free cholesterol in bile
- convert to [[bile acid]], [[bile salt]]

**excresion** in feces, after secreted in bile 


disease
- Smith-Lemli-opitz syndrome (SLOS) - cholesterol deficiency
- familial LCAT deficeincy - decreased HDL -> dyslipidemia -> cardiovascular disease
- excessive ACAT and cholesterol -> cardiovascular disease
- bile salt deficiency -> decreased cholesterol solubility in bile -> fat malabsorbtion (steatorrhea) -> deficiency of fat-soluble vitamins -> cholesterol saturation -> gallstone
