layers (outer to inner)
- *stratum corneum* - cornified cells
- *stratum lucidum* - clear cells, 
- *stratum granulosum* - granular cells
- *stratum spinosum* - spinous cells
- *stratum basale* - basal cells
*stratum lucidum* is absense in thin skin
*stratum granulosum* has 3-5 layers in thick skin, fragmented 1 layer in thin skin

cell type
- [[keratinocyte]] - found mostly in all 5 layers
	- produce keratin
	- brick(cell)-and-mortar(lipids)
- **non-keratinocyte**
	- [[melanocyte]] - *stratum basale*
		- produce melanin granules, released via dendrites
	- [[Langerhans celI]] - *stratum basale/spinosum*
		- antigen presenting cell
		- no desmosome, use E-cadherin
	- [[Merkel cell]] - *stratum basale*
		- mechanoreceptor
		- linked to nerve plate in basal lamina
	- **epidermal stem cell**

function
- protect against germ
- hold moisture
- skin color (melanin)
