adenosine triphosphate
ATP hydrolysis 
- remove phosphate: ATP -> ADP + phosphate -> AMP + pyrophosphate
- give out energy (-G)

ATP can be obtained by
- [[substrate-level phosphorylation]] - directly transfer phosphate from 'phosphagen molecule' to ADP
- [[oxidative-level phosphorylation]] - use proton gradient to spin ATPsynthase that adds inorganic phosphate to ADP
