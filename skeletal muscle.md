	- perimysium - cover muscle fascicle
	- endomysium - cover muscle fiber ([[reticular fiber]])

features
- long, cylindrical cell, no branching
- **multi-nucleated** at peripheral
- **striated** cytoplasm (striped)
- *Triad* appearance
	- 1 T-tubule, formed from cell membrane (sarcolemma)
	- 2 sarcoplasmic reticulum terminals
#### muscle fiber

development
- many myoblasts fused together -> myotube -> muscle fiber 
- myosatellite cell - postnatal skeletal maintenance, repair, regeneration
