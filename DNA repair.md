usually occur in S, G2 phase to fix double-strand breaks, collapsed replication fork
use sister chromatid as template

methods: 
- [[homologous recombination]] - double-strand break
- [[non-homologous end joining]] - double-strand break
- [[base excision repair]] - single-strand break
- [[nucleotide excision repair]] - pyrimidine dimer
- [[mismatch repair]] - base mismatch, insertion, deletion
- [[SOS repair]]

inherited syndromes with defective DNA repair
- xeroderma pigmentosum
	- cause skin cancer, UV sensitivity
- BRCA1 and BRCA2 mutation
	- cause breast, ovarian cancer
