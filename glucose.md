major energy source
- may converted to [[glycogen]] or [[lipid]]

[[glycogenolysis]] vs [[gluconeogenesis]] 

| phase (hrs)     | origin of glucose                      |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| I (0-4)         | exogenous (eat food)                   |
| II (4-16)       | **glycogen** + hepatic gluconeogenesis |
| III (16-28)     | glycogen +** hepatic gluconeogenesis** |
| IV (28-24x24)   | renal & hepatic gluconeogenesis        |
| V (24x24-40x24) | renal & hepatic gluconeogenesis        |

#### glucose transportation

- SGLT (sodium glucose linked transporter) - [[secondary active transport]]

| type  | found in                            | function                           |
| ----- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| SGLT1 | small intestine an renal tubules    | absorbs digested glucose/galactose |
| SGLT2 | kidney's proximal convoluted tubule | reabsorbs filtered glucose         |

- GLUT (glucose transporter) - [[facilitated diffusion]]

| type  | found in                             | Km, affinity        | function                                                                          |
| ----- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GLUT1 | RBC, blood-brain barrier             | 1 mM, high affinity | easy uptake                                                                       |
| GLUT2 | liver, pancreatic beta cell, nephron | 15 mM, low affinity | uptake when high glucose                                                          |
| GLUT3 | all tissue, neuron                   | 1 mM, high affinity | easy uptake                                                                       |
| GLUT4 | skeletal muscle, adipose tissue      | 5 mM                | *insulin-dependent*, works when transport protein vesicle fuse with cell membrane |
| GLUT5 |                                      |                     | fructose transport                                                                |
