
mineral in body
- macroelements (major) eg. Ca, P, Na, K, Cl, Mg, S
- microelements (trace) eg. Fe, I, Zn, Cu

function
- body structure (calcium)
- cofactor
- electrolyte balance 
	- more Mg, K inside cell
	- more Na, Cl outside cell

factor affecting mineral bioavailability
- other mineral
- phytic acid, oxalic acid found in plant can bind to minerals -> lower availability
- polyphenol -> lower availability
- gastric acidity -> increase availability (Fe)

### macroelements
#### calcium
found in bone in form of calcium hydroxyapatite
forms
- ionized (Ca2+) - active, found in this form highly in acidosis
- bound - inactive, found in this form highly in alkalosis
- complexed - inactive

function
- neurotransmitter release
- muscle contraction
- coagulating factor
- second messenger

absorption
- absorbed in small intestine
	- vitamin D stimulate calcium transporter (VDR) -> increase absorption
- phytate, oxalalate decrease absorption

homeostasis
- low calcium
	- [[parathyroid hormone]] increase calcium
		- bone resorption (to blood)
		- activate [[vitamin D]] at kidney
		- increase absorption at intestine

clinical
- hypocalcemia
	- [[osteoporosis]]
	- neuromuscular irritability -> tetany, carpopedal spasm, etc.
- hypercalcemia

#### phosphorus
in form of phosphate
function:
- component of ATP, creatine phosphate
- component of DNA, RNA, phospholipid, etc.
- buffer system
- control enzymes

clinical
- hyperphosphatemia
	- chronic kidney disease

#### magnesium
found in chlorophyll
function:
- coenzyme eg. phosphofructokinase, Na-K ATPase, etc.
- neuromuscular transmission, muscle contraction

clinical
- hypomagnesemia
	- neuromuscular excitability
	- arrhythmia
	- hypocalcemia, hypokalemia(K)
#### sodium
most abundant cation(+) in extracellular cellular matrix
regulation
- [[renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system]] 

function
- nerve action potential
- sodium-glucose cotransport
- maintain osmotic equilibrium
- increase blood pressure

clinical
- hyponatremia
	- cause eg. [[Addison's disease]] (hypoaldosteronism)
	- cell swelling -> brain edema
- hypernatremia
	- neuropathy

#### postassium
involved in hydrogen-potassium ATPase (bring K+ in, H+ out)
acidosis -> less H+ out -> less K+ in -> more K+ in blood
alkalosis -> more H+ out -> more K+ in -> less K+ in blood

function:
- hydrogen-potassium ATPase release acid in gastric parietal cell
- nerve action potential
- cofactor of enzymes eg. pyruvate kinase, aldolase, ATPase

clinical
- hypokalemia
	- cause eg. hyperaldosteronism
	- muscle weakness, instestinal muscle dysfunction, cardiac arrythmia
- hyperkalemia
	- cause eg. hemolysis, acidosis

### trace elements

#### iron
component of heme 
forms: Fe2+ (ferrous ion), Fe3+ (ferric ion)
function
- carry oxygen in heme
- component of enzymes eg. cytochrome oxidase, peroxidase, catalase
- transport-protein eg. transferrin, ferroportin
- storage - ferritin, hemosiredin
vitamin C helps iron absorption

hepcidin - inhibit ferroportin reabsorbing iron
- low -> more iron absorption (hemochromatosis)
- high -> low absorption (iron deficiency/anemia)

clinical
- iron deficiency -> [[anemia]]
	- low serum ferritin, low serum iron, high transferin
- iron overload (caused by hepcidin deficiency)
	- [[hemochromatosis]]
		- primary hemochromatosis - hereditary
		- secondary hemochromatosis - chronic blood transfusion
	- free radical
treatment eg. iron chelator

#### copper
involved in redox reactions eg. cytochrome C oxidase

clinical 
- excess copper
	- [[Wilson disease]]
		- caused by mutation in copper-transporting gene ATP78
		- copper accumulation in eye (K-F ring), liver

#### zinc
function
- cofactor of enzymes eg. alcohol dehydrogenase, carbonic anhydrase
- antioxidant
- reproductive hormones
- component of insulin
- formation of collagen

clinical
- zinc deficiency
	- eczema

#### selenium
function
- antioxidant
	- component of glutathione peroxidase

#### iodine
function
- component of thyroid hormones
clinical
- iondine deficiency
	- [[cretinism]]
	- [[goiter]]

#### fluorine
function
- support teeth
	- forms hydroxyfluroapatite crystals
