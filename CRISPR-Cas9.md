CRISPR is a natural bacterial immune system
when bacteriophage infect bacteria, bacteria clip out piece of virus's DNA and store in genome as memory. for next attack, bacteria transcribe that into guide RNA and *Cas9* uses that guide to find and destroy viral DNA.

repurpose for gene editing
- *single-guide RNA* guides Cas9 to cutting gene at precise location
	- after cutting, the cell repairs via [[homologous recombination]] (for gene correction) or [[non-homologous end joining]] (for gene disruption)
