absorption in intestine
occurs mostly in liver

1 standard drink approx. 14 grams of ethanol
standard level: <50 mg/dL

toxicity to liver:
- acetaldehyde accumulation 
	- genetic variations in Asian reduce ALDH activity -> acetaldehyde can't turn into acetate
- ROS formation (via minor pathway)
- higher NADH/NAD+ ratio
	- [[hyperlipidemia]] 
	- [[hyperuricemia]]
	- ketoacidosis

effect:
- [[thiamine(B1) deficiency]] - Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome
	- B1 used in pyruvate dehydrogenase and alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase
- brain damage
- cardiovascular diseases
- fetal alcohol syndrome
- cancer
- drug interactions
	- paracetamol - CYP2E1 turns paracetamol to NAPQI (toxic) -> get rid by glutathione conjugation
- methanol toxicity
	- methanol -> formaldehyde (toxic)
	- cause retina damage, blindness
