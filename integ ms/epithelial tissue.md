
#### features:
- derived from 3 germ layers 
	- endoderm: lining of hollow organs; RS, GI, etc.
	- mesoderm: lining of blood vessels
	- ectoderm: epidermis, mammary gland
- avascular tissue
- anchored by [[basal lamina]] (part of [[basement membrane]])
- cover surfaces except articular surface, enamel, anterior of iris

#### specialization
- **apical domain**
	- [[microvilli]]
	- [[stereocilia]]
	- [[cilia]]
- lateral domain
	- [[tight junction]] 
	- [[adeherens junction]]
	- [[desmosome junction]]
	- [[gap junction]]
- basal domain
	- basal infoldings (striation)
	- [[hemidesmosome junction]]

#### classification
- [[covering epithelium]]
	- ##### simple (1 layer)
		- simple squamous eg. alveoli, capillary
		- simple cuboidal eg. kidney, ducts
		- simple columnar eg. instestinal lining
		- pseudostratified 
	- ##### stratified (many layers)
		- stratified squamous
		- stratified cuboidal
		- stratified columnar
		- transitional or pseudostratified , found in urinary bladder
- **glandular epithelium**
	- [[endocrine gland]] - secrete directly into blood
	- [[exocrine gland]] - secrete via duct

**special epithelium**
- olfactory epithelium
- taste bud
- organ of Corti
- rods and cones in retina
- macula, utricle, crista ampullaris
- [[myoepithelial cell]]

epithelioid tissue

clinical
- carcinoma
- metaplasia - eg. transformation from squamous to columnar
