hollow tube consisting of 13 columns of [[tubulin]] dimer (alpha and beta)

functions:
- maintain cell shape
- [[cell motility]] eg. [[cilia]] and [[flagella]]
- **chromosome separation in cell division** (forming [[spindle fiber]])
- organelle movement

structure:
- consists of **alpha-tublin** and **beta-tubulin**
	- linear chain of subunits called **protofilament**
- ends
	- **minus end** points toward center
	- **plus end** points toward peripheral
- continually gain and lose subunits 

polymerization:
- GTP bound to **beta-tubulin** is exchangeable (can connect to another dimer)
- GTP dimer bond to end of microtubule
	- GTP hydrolyzes becoming GDP 
		- GDP dimer lock in place (but easily break apart at ends)
			- GTP cap stop GDP dimer from breaking

dynamic instability:
- catastrophe
	- lost of GTP cap cause rapid disassembly
- rescue
	- regain GTP cap and continue growing

microtubule organizing center [[centrosome]]  or [[basal body]]
- starting point of microtubule (minus end)

microtubule associated [[motor protein]]:
- [[kinesin]] - anterograde transport from - to + (outward)
- [[dynein]] - retrograde transport from + to - (inward)
