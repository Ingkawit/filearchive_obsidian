long hydrocarbon chain with terminal carboxylic group
types
- saturated - no double bond
	- eg. palmitic, steric
	- high melting point -> solid at room temp.
- unsaturated - contain double bond
	- low melting point -> liquid at room temp.
	- types:
		- monounstaurated (MUFA)
		- polyunsaturated (PUFA)

position:
- 𝛂 (2nd carbon / 1st carbon exclude carboxylic group)
- 𝛃 (3nd carbon / 2st carbon exclude carboxylic group)
- 𝝎 (last carbon)

synthesis - in cytosol of hepatocyte, adipocyte etc.
- rate-limiting step: acetyl-coA carboxylase (acetyl coA -> melanyl coA)
	- coenzyme: biotin (B7)
- final product: palmitate (C16:0)

elongation - at ER
- use malonyl coA to add 2C unit
- desaturation : mammal cannot insert double bond beyond c9
	- essential FA - must eat from diet
