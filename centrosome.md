- [[microtubule]] organizing center for mitotic spindle during cell division
structure:
- bound pair of [[centriole]]s angled 90degrees
- each centriole consist of 9 microtubular triplets
functions:
- in cell division, it duplicates and moves to opposite sides
- microtubule proteins assemble into spindle (kinetochore microtubule attaches to [[kinetochore]], astral microtubule attaches to nothing)
