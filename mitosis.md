mitotic phase
- **prophase**
	- intact nuclear envelope, condensing chromosome
	- centrosomes move apart, forming spindle microtubules
- **pro-metaphase**
	- fragmented nuclear envelope, scattered chromosome
	- centrosome at opposite pole, spindle microtubules attach to kinetochores
- **metaphase**
	- chromosomes align at equator
		- kinetochore microtubules - attached to sister chromatid
		- astral microtubules - not attached
		- interpolar microtubules - two microtubules interact each other
- ==---M checkpoint (all chromosomes attached to spindle?)==
- **anaphase**
	- sister chromatids separate forming 2 daughter chromosomes
	- shortening of kinetochore microtubule (microtubule catastrophe)
- **telophase**
	- daughter chromosomes at opposite poles
	- new nuclear envelope starts forming
	- assembly of contractile ring (actin filament) on plasma membrane
- **cytokinesis**
	- formed nuclei
	- decondensed chromosomes
	- cytoplasm divided by contractile ring of actin and myosin
**G0** - resting/quiescent state found in terminally differentiated cells

cell cycle regulatory proteins
- [[cyclin]] and [[CDK]]s combination let cell pass through checkpoints
	- cyclin binds to CDK to partially activate
	- CDK-activating kinase (CAK) phosphorylates cyclin-CDK complexes to fully activate
		* there can be '*inhibitory phosphate*' that will deactivate if phosphorylated (by Wee1 kinase)
	* CDK inhibitor (p16,p21,p27) bind to cyclin-CDK to inhibit -> proteolysis (to destroy cyclin)
