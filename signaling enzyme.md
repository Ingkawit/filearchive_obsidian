phosphorylation -> conformational change -> change in catalytic activity 


examples:
- [[kinase]] turn on other proteins by phosphorylation 
	- signaling by phosphorylation
		turn on protein
		- kinase phosphorylates enzyme 
		turn off protein
		- phosphatase removes phosphate from protein
	- ** phosphatase can both turn on in some cases
- [[G-protein]] (GTPase)  binds to other proteins to turn on
	- signaling by GTP binding
		turn on G-protein 
		- GTP binding (GDP -> GTP)
		turn off G-protein 
		- GTP hydrolysis (GTP -> GDP)

double negative signal
- kinase inhibits inhibitor, which inhibits transcription regulator, which promote gene expression
