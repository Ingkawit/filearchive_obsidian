how to get lysosomal hydrolases to lysosome
1. lysosomal hydrolases are synthesized in [[endoplasmic reticulum]] and move to cis Golgi
2. lysosomal hydrolases are added with [[mannose-6-phosphate]] that binds to a receptor 
3.  lysosomal hydrolases with M6P receptors move into vesicle
4. vesicle fuses with lysosome
