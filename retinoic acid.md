vital in development

retinol -> retinoic acid
retinoic acid activates **RAR**, **RXR**  receptor on transcription factor

deficiency:
- squamous metaplasia - epithelium produces keratin, hard and dry
	- keratomalacia (corneal melting) - keratin produced in corneal
	- Bitot's spot - white spot in conjunctiva
	- airwary epithelium squamous metaplasia - prone to infection
	- follicular keratosis - skin
toxicity:
- teratogenicity - embryo malformation
	- retinoic acid can be teratogenic during pregnancy
	- cause child facial anomaly, thymic agenesis, neurodevelopmental disability

**isotretinoin** - synthesized retinoic acid derivative 
- binds to RAR transcription factor
- triggers apoptosis in 
	- sebocyte -> sebum supression -> acne treatment
	- neural crest cell -> teratogenicity
