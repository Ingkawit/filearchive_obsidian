large protein attached to short, branched sugar chain
- non-enzymatic glycated proteins eg. used to track blood glucose levels
	- glycated albumin
	- hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c)
- enzymatic glycation (glycosylation)
	- N-linked glycosylation - amino group of Asn 
		- N-acetylglucosamine
	- O-linked glycosylation - hydroxyl group of Ser/Thr
		- N-acetylgalactosamine
