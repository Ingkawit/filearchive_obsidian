


means of secretion
- merocrine (eccrine) - release only product via exocytosis
	- eg. [[sweat gland]], [[pancreas]], [[mammary gland]]
- apocrine - release product mixed with cytoplasm in vesicle
	- eg. [[mammary gland]]
- holocrine (involve [[apoptosis]]) - discharge all products
	- eg. [[sebaceous gland]]
