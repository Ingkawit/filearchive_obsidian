converting [[messenger RNA]] to sequence of amino acids (as part of protein synthesis)

 [[ribosome]]
 [[transfer RNA]]

steps:
0. synthesis of aminoacyl-tRNA
	- aminoacyl-tRNA synthase create ester bond between *carboxylic group of specific amino acid* with *3' OH group of tRNA*
1. **initiation**
	- **initiation factors and initiator tRNA** (with methionine in euk., formylmethioine in prok.) bind into ribosome's **P site**
	- prok.
		- ribosome binds at *Shine-Dalgarno sequence* (upstream of AUG)
	- euk.
		- 5'cap of mRNA recruits ribosome and *Kozak sequence* help recognize AUG
2. **elongation**
	- next **tRNA with elongation factor** binds at **A site**
	- amino acids from P site move to bond with new amino acid in A site forming **peptidyl tRNA** (C-terminus attached to tRNA)
	- ribosome slide along mRNA in 5' to 3' direction
		- peptidyl tRNA moves from A site to P site
		- empty tRNA moves from P site to E site and exit
3. **termination**
	- when reached stop codon, **release factor** binds at P site instead of tRNA
	- everthing dissociates

mutation effects:
- frameshift proteins
- triggers [[protein degradation]]

protein folding:
- newly synthesized protein folded by Hsp70
- if folding incorrect, Hsp60 correct
followed by glycosylation, phosphorylation, acetylation in Golgi apparatus
