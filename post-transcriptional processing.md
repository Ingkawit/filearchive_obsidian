[[RNA polymerase]] II carries post-translational proteins on its tail
steps:
1. **5' capping** - to stabilize mRNA, facilitate the binding of mRNA to ribosome subunit
	- add 7-methyl-quanosine (m7G) at first nucleotide of 5' end
2. [[splicing]] - to remove introns and join exons together
	- spliceosomes catalyze splicing
	- self-splicing, introns loop called lariat 
3. **3' polyadenylation (poly-A tail)** - to stabilize mRNA, facilitate the binding of mRNA to ribosome subunit
