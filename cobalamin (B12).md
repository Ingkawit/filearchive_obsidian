not found in plants

metabolism
1. B12 binds with R factor in saliva to protect from stomach acid
2. intrinsic factor (IF) is synthesize in parietal cell in gastric mucosa
	- AutoAb (pernicious) destroys parietal cell -> IF defieciency
3. in duodenum, pancreatic enzyme cleaves off R factor, B12 binds with IF instead
	- chronic pancreatitis -> B12 malabsorption
	- bacterial overgrowth destroys B12-IF complex
	- fish tapeworms use B12 -> deficiency
4. B12-IF binds to receptors in terminal ilium
	- Crohn's disease (terminal ileum) causes B12 malabsorption

functions:
- methylation reaction
- coenzyme of methylmelonyl coA
